Storage engineered for privacy

Everything you expect from modern cloud storage, without the compromise.

Zero-knowledge encryption

Your files are encrypted before they leave your device. We can't see your data, ever. Our open-source cryptographic architecture guarantees total privacy.

Lightning-fast block sync

Modify a huge file? We only upload the bytes that changed. Experience instantaneous syncing across all your linked macOS, Windows, iOS, and Android devices.

Secure granular sharing

Create public links with passwords and expiration dates. Or invite team members to collaborate on folders with granular read, write, and manage permissions.

Infinite version history

Accidentally overwrote a document? Ransomware hit your local drive? Roll back any file or entire folder to a previous state with a single click, up to 365 days.
